## PROMPT ÚNICO DE IMAGENS (colar no Google Flow, Agent Mode, modelo Nano Banana, 16:9)

Generate 10 images, one per scene, ALL in this exact locked style: Editorial paper-collage
illustration on a warm cream paper background, flat muted colors, paper-cutout characters and
objects with subtle drop shadows, ONE deep crimson red accent element per scene, occasional
black-and-white halftone photographic collage elements mixed in, fine paper grain, minimalist
composition with generous empty space, no text, no letters, no words.

Scene 1: US Marines paper cutouts stepping from landing boats onto a pale beach, a black-and-white halftone photographic camera on a tripod watching them from the sand, crimson red sun on the horizon.
Scene 2: A vintage 1960s television set as paper cutout, inside its screen the beach landing scene appears, living room silhouettes watching, crimson red glow from the screen.
Scene 3: A huge paper map of the ocean, a long line of tiny paper-cutout soldiers crossing it like a bridge from one continent to another, one crimson red arrow pointing across.
Scene 4: Cross-section of jungle earth in paper layers showing tunnels beneath the surface, tiny paper-cutout fighters inside the tunnels, dense paper-cut jungle above, one crimson red lantern glowing.
Scene 5: A dark paper city map with one hundred small crimson red explosion bursts of torn paper appearing across it, paper smoke rising.
Scene 6: The same vintage TV cutout now cracked, a black-and-white halftone photographic anchorman face on screen, crimson red graph line falling across the screen.
Scene 7: A cozy paper-cutout living room, family silhouettes on a sofa, the TV glowing with a battle scene, the crimson red light spilling across the floor like a stain.
Scene 8: A large paper document with a black-and-white halftone photographic handshake on it, in the background paper helicopters lifting from a rooftop, one crimson red flag rising over the city.
Scene 9: A vast field of small white paper markers in rows fading to the horizon on cream paper, one crimson red flower cutout in the front row.
Scene 10: A giant black-and-white halftone photographic eye reflected inside a TV screen, jungle silhouette fading inside the reflection, crimson red scribbled circle drawn around the screen.

## PROMPT ÚNICO DE ANIMAÇÃO (colar depois no Flow, Agent Mode → animar as 10 imagens)

Animate all 10 generated images, one clip each, 5 seconds, in this locked motion style:
subtle stop-motion paper collage feel, gentle continuous camera drift, elements move softly
(figures walk in place, smoke rises, screens flicker, flags wave, paper edges tremble slightly),
no morphing, no new elements, no text, preserve the exact illustration style and colors.
Clip 1: the marines step forward as the camera slowly pushes in; Clip 2: the TV screen flickers
and the scene inside moves; Clip 3: the line of soldiers marches across the map along the red
arrow; Clip 4: lantern light flickers in the tunnels as figures move through them; Clip 5: the
red bursts pop up one after another across the map; Clip 6: the red graph line falls as the
screen static shivers; Clip 7: the TV light pulses over the family as shadows shift; Clip 8:
helicopters lift as the red flag rises; Clip 9: slow drift over the field of markers toward the
red flower; Clip 10: the eye blinks once inside the screen as the red circle draws itself around it.
